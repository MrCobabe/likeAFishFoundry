Like a Fish Foundry

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Overview

Like a Fish Foundry respects your privacy. This policy explains what information may be collected when you use likeafishfoundry.com, request a custom casting quote, upload a model file, or interact with features connected to our eBay storefront, and how that information is used and protected.

Information You Provide

When you request a custom casting quote, we may collect information you choose to provide, including your name, email address, requested quantity, project details, and an STL model file. We use this information to review your project, prepare or discuss a quote, communicate with you, and provide the service you requested.

STL File Uploads and Retention

STL files submitted through the quote form are stored in private cloud storage and are not intended for public access. Quote-request metadata associated with an upload may also be stored so that we can identify and respond to the request. Our current system is configured to automatically delete these uploaded files and associated quote-request records after approximately 90 days. Files may also be deleted earlier when they are no longer needed.

eBay Integration

Like a Fish Foundry may use eBay developer services to retrieve and display information related to our own eBay seller listings, such as listing titles, prices, categories, photographs, availability, and links to eBay. Purchases made through eBay are completed on eBay and are subject to eBay's own terms and privacy practices.

We do not ask visitors to provide their eBay passwords to Like a Fish Foundry. If an eBay authorization feature is used, authentication is handled through eBay's authorization system. We request only permissions reasonably needed for the features we operate.

How We Use Information

Information we receive is used to operate the website and foundry services, respond to inquiries and quote requests, maintain and improve the storefront, synchronize our eBay listings, protect the security and reliability of our systems, and comply with applicable legal or platform requirements.

Sharing and Sale of Information

Like a Fish Foundry does not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that support the operation of the website and its features, such as website hosting, cloud storage, email delivery, and eBay's services. We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect rights or security, or respond to a valid legal requirement.

Data Security

We use reasonable technical measures intended to protect information handled by the site. For example, the custom casting upload system uses HTTPS, private cloud storage, and time-limited upload and download authorization. No method of internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Account Deletion and Platform Notifications

Where a connected platform such as eBay sends a valid account-deletion or account-closure notification, we will handle information covered by that request in accordance with applicable platform requirements and our data-retention obligations. Information that is no longer necessary for the purposes for which it was collected may be deleted.

Your Choices

You may contact Like a Fish Foundry if you have a question about information you submitted through this website or want to request correction or deletion of information that we still retain, subject to any legal or operational retention requirements.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the website, services, or legal and platform requirements change. The date at the top of this page identifies the latest revision.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Like a Fish Foundry using the contact information provided on the main website.
